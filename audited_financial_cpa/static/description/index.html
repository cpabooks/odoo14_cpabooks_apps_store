Management-prepared statements

These statements are prepared from the accounting records in Odoo. They are management-prepared financial statements. They are not an independent external audit and they are not an auditor's opinion. Where a statutory audit is required, the audit report is issued by the appointed external auditor. The module does not replace the accounting system, and balances do not have to be typed in again.
